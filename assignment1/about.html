About KBT College of Engineering

About the College

MVPS Karmaveer Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering (KBTCOE), Nashik, was established to provide quality engineering education. The institute is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University and approved by AICTE.

The college provides modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories, digital classrooms, research facilities, and industry-oriented education.

Vision

To become a premier institute for engineering education by developing competent professionals with ethical values.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Develop leadership and professional ethics.
  • Enhance industry interaction.

Facilities

  • Central Library
  • Computer Labs
  • Hostel
  • Sports Complex
  • Cafeteria
  • Wi-Fi Campus
