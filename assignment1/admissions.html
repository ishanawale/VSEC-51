Admissions

Admission Process

Admissions are carried out through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Government of Maharashtra.

Eligibility

  • Passed HSC (Science).
  • Qualified MHT-CET or JEE Main.
  • Documents verified through CAP.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • MHT-CET/JEE Scorecard
  • Leaving Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size Photographs

Courses Offered

  • Computer Engineering
  • Information Technology
  • AI & Data Science
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • ENTC Engineering
